# Week 9 — Combined configurations and interactions

## Learning targets

- Test a matrix of combined aero packages.
- Recognize **interactions**: parts do not always add linearly.
- Select a “recommended design” using an explicit metric (e.g., maximize \(|C_L|\) subject to \(C_D\) cap, or maximize \(|C_L|/C_D\)).

## Physics checkpoint

1. Suppose wing-only adds \(\Delta F_z = -1.2\,\mathrm{N}\) and diffuser-only adds \(\Delta F_z = -0.8\,\mathrm{N}\), but both together add \(\Delta F_z = -1.5\,\mathrm{N}\). Are the effects additive? What might that imply?  
2. Propose a decision rule for the “best” student design given your project goals (design brief from Week 1).  
3. List two sources of systematic error that could fake an interaction.

- Start final figures (clear labels, units, Re or speed noted).
