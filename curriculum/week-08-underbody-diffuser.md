# Week 8 — Splitters, diffusers, and underbody flow

## Learning targets

- Explain splitter intent: manage front stagnation / underbody mass flow (at a conceptual level).
- Explain diffuser intent: expand/slow underbody flow and recover pressure; relate to ride height sensitivity.
- Test at least two underbody configurations and compare vertical load and drag.

## Physics checkpoint

1. If underbody flow slows in a diffuser and pressure rises toward the exit, how can that still be compatible with **low pressure under the car** helping downforce? (Think about the whole underbody path, not one equation in isolation.)  
2. Why is ride height / ground clearance critical for these devices?  
3. Name one measurement that would help distinguish “more downforce” from “more drag for little gain.”

## Design / build tasks

- Build splitter and diffuser variants (or block-off plates as controls).
- Keep body + wing fixed while swapping underbody parts (single-variable tests).
- Record ride height carefully—small changes matter.

## Deliverables

- [ ] Config photos with scale ruler for ride height  
- [ ] Comparison table: baseline vs. splitter vs. diffuser vs. both  
- [ ] Physics interpretation paragraph avoiding pure Bernoulli slogans

## Mentor notes

Expect messy real data. Reward clean experimental controls over flashy claims.
