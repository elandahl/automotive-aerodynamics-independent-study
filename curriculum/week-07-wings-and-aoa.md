# Week 7 — Wings, angle of attack, and stall (applied)

## Learning targets

- Define geometric angle of attack for the modular wing.
- Predict qualitative $C_L$ and $C_D$ vs. AoA: rise, peak, stall/separation.
- Collect a real AoA sweep and plot coefficients vs. AoA.

## Physics checkpoint

1. Sketch expected $C_L(\alpha)$ and $C_D(\alpha)$ for a simple cambered wing; mark a stall region.  
2. Why might a race-car wing run at angles that look “too aggressive” compared to aircraft cruise?  
3. If downforce rises 40% but drag rises 80%, how did downforce-to-drag change?

## Design / build tasks

- Machine/print AoA adjustment with labeled detents (e.g., 0°, 5°, 10°, 15°).
- Run AoA sweep at fixed fan setting; ≥3 replicates each.
- Optional: photograph tufts or use incense/fog for separation visualization (careful with sensors/fire safety).

## Deliverables

- [ ] Data CSV using [labs/data-template.csv](../labs/data-template.csv)  
- [ ] Plot of $C_L,C_D$ vs. AoA  
- [ ] Short interpretation (½–1 page)

## Mentor notes

If the wing stalls early on the scale model, connect back to Re (Week 5)—small models often separate earlier.
