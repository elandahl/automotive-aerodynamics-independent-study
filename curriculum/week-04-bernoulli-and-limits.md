# Week 4 — Bernoulli, continuity, and the limits of “fast air = low pressure”

## Learning targets

- State continuity for incompressible flow: \(A v \approx \text{constant}\) along a streamline tube.
- Write Bernoulli’s equation (steady, inviscid, incompressible along a streamline) and compute a sample \(\Delta P\).
- Explain **why cars are hard**: separation, wakes, and bluff bodies mean Bernoulli-only stories often fail.

## Physics checkpoint

1. Air in a duct speeds from 10 to 20 m/s. Using Bernoulli (same height), estimate \(\Delta P\). Is pressure higher where the air is slower or faster?  
2. A diffuser **slows** underbody flow as area increases. In a Bernoulli-only story, what should happen to pressure? How does that relate to “pressure recovery”?  
3. Give one reason a real rear wing’s downforce is **not** fully explained by “air travels farther over the top.”  
4. *Stretch:* List three assumptions of Bernoulli and mark which fail for a sharp-edged spoiler in a stalled condition.

- Identify likely separation edges on the scale body.
