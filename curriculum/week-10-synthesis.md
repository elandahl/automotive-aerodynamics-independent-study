# Week 10 — Synthesis, final report, and presentation

## Learning targets

- Tell a coherent story: design question → physics model → experiment → result → redesign insight.
- Communicate uncertainty and scaling limits without undermining the real learning.
- Reflect on Physics minor readiness: what to take next.

## Final report structure (suggested)

1. **Design brief** — problem and success metrics  
2. **Physics framework** — forces, $q$, coefficients, Re disclaimer  
3. **Apparatus** — model scale, sensors, calibration, protocol  
4. **Results** — AoA sweep; underbody tests; combined configs (plots + tables)  
5. **Discussion** — what matched predictions; what surprised; interactions  
6. **Design recommendations** — what to keep/change in the modular kit  
7. **Personal learning** — physics concepts mastered; next courses  

Target length: ~8–15 pages including figures (quality over bulk).

## Presentation (10–12 minutes)

- 1 min: design goal  
- 3 min: physics ideas that mattered  
- 4 min: key plots  
- 2 min: recommendations + scaling honesty  
- Q&A

## Deliverables

- [ ] Final report in `docs/final-report.md` (or PDF)  
- [ ] Presentation slides  
- [ ] Repo cleanup: labeled data, CAD export notes, README links updated  
- [ ] Brief reflection on Physics minor pathway

## Celebration check

The win is not a perfect wind-tunnel match to F1. The win is **using physics to drive design decisions** and stating evidence clearly.
