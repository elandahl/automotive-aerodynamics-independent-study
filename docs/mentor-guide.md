# Mentor guide (faculty)

Private-ish checklist for running the independent study. Safe to keep public; contains no grades.

## Core pedagogical bet

The student learns physics by **predicting → measuring → revising design**. CAD without force models will not meet the Physics IS bar; force models without a physical artifact will not meet the Industrial Design motivation. Hold both.

## Weekly rhythm (suggested)

1. 20–30 min physics checkpoint discussion  
2. 20–30 min build/data review  
3. Agree on next week’s single main variable  

## Common misconceptions to correct early

| Heard | Redirect |
|-------|----------|
| Downforce makes the car lighter | Increases normal force; mass unchanged |
| Fast air always low pressure | Only under Bernoulli assumptions; wakes differ |
| Scale $C_D$ = full-scale $C_D$ | Discuss Re (Week 5) |
| More aero parts always better | Interactions + drag cost |

## Optional enrichment (Physics minor)

- Logarithmic plots of force vs. speed to see $v^2$ scaling  
- Simple Python/`spreadsheet` pipeline from CSV → $C_D,C_L$  
- Order-of-magnitude comparison to published student formula SAE data (with skepticism)

## Checkpoint solutions

Outline solutions live with the student when possible. If you want a written key in-repo later, add `curriculum/answer-keys/` (gitignore if you prefer closed keys).

## Grading tip

Reward: clear FBDs, defined $A$, Re disclaimer, clean single-variable tests, honest negative results.  
Do not overweight: glossy CAD renders without data.
